# Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo

Kugcine ukubuyekezwa: 25 Septhemba 2026

**BUtil** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Igama lohlelo: BUtil
Igama lomthuthukisi: Siarhei Kuchuk

I-BUtil igcina amakhophi, ihlanganisa, ibuye ibuyisele amafayela kule khompyutha. Ingangenisa nemidiya, yabelane ngefolda, noma ilayishe amafayela kuseva oyisethayo. Ayidali i-akhawunti yomthuthukisi. Umthuthukisi akasebenzisi iseva ethola amafayela akho, amaphasiwedi, noma idatha yokusetshenziswa.

## Idatha umthuthukisi angayiqoqi

Uhlelo aluna zikhangiso, ukuhlaziywa, imibiko yokuphahlazeka, noma ama-SDK okulandelela. Umthuthukisi akaqoqi, athengise, noma abelane ngedatha yomuntu siqu.

## Idatha egcinwe kukhompyutha yakho

### Imisebenzi nezilungiselelo

Izincazelo zemisebenzi zigcinwa kule khompyutha kuphela. Umsebenzi ungaba nezindlela zamafolda, ishejuli, izilungiselelo zokugcina, namaphasiwedi noma amathokheni owathayiphayo. Amaphasiwedi nezimfihlo zokugcina zifihlwa ngokubhala kule khompyutha ngaphambi kokugcinwa, futhi zingafundwa kule khompyutha kuphela. Lawo manani awathunyelwa kumthuthukisi.

- Imisebenzi ye-Windows: `%AppData%\BUtil Backup Tasks`
- Imisebenzi ye-Linux: `~/.config/BUtil Backup Tasks`
- Izilungiselelo ze-Windows (kuhlanganise itimu nolimi olukhethwe kokugcina kwelayisensi noma ubumfihlo): `%AppData%\BUtil\Settings\v1`
- Izilungiselelo ze-Linux: `~/.config/BUtil/Settings/v1`
- Isimo semisebenzi ye-Windows: `%AppData%\BUtil\States`
- Isimo semisebenzi ye-Linux: `~/.config/BUtil/States`
- Isimo sokungenisa imidiya ye-Windows: `%AppData%\BUtil Backup Tasks - States`
- Isimo sokungenisa imidiya ye-Linux: `~/.config/BUtil Backup Tasks - States`

### Amafayela owakhethayo

Ukwenza ikhophi, ukuhlanganisa, ukubuyisela, nokungenisa kufunda futhi kubhale amafolda owakhethayo. Lawo mafayela ahlala kule khompyutha noma endaweni yokugcina oyisethayo. Uhlelo aluwathumeli kumthuthukisi.

### Amalogi

Amalogi okuxilonga abhalwa kule khompyutha kuphela:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\BUtil\logs\v4`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/BUtil/logs/v4`

Lawo mafayela awathunyelwa ndawo.

Asikho iseva yomthuthukisi esetshenziselwa ukugcina idatha yakho.

## Ukusetshenziswa kwenethiwekhi

### Izindawo ozisethayo

Uma umsebenzi usebenza, uhlelo luxhuma kuphela endaweni oyibekayo. Kungaba ifolda yendawo noma iseva oyifakayo: FTP, FTPS, SFTP, WebDAV, SMB, NFS, ukugcina okuhambisana ne-S3, noma i-Azure Blob Storage. Amagama amafayela, okuqukethwe, nezitifiketi ozifakile zithunyelwa kuleyo seva ukuze umsebenzi ukwazi ukusebenza. Inkonzo ngayinye inenqubomgomo yayo yobumfihlo. Umthuthukisi akayitholi leyo thrafikhi.

I-BUtil Server ingalalela kule khompyutha ukuze iklayenti ye-BUtil oyisethayo ikwazi ukuthumela amafayela. Leyo thrafikhi ihlala phakathi kwamakhompyutha owamisayo.

### Ukuhlola izibuyekezo

Izakhiwo ezingaphandle kwe-Store zingacela ukukhishwa kwakamuva kwe-GitHub:

`https://api.github.com/repos/drweb86/butil/releases/latest`

I-GitHub (Microsoft) ithola isicelo se-HTTPS esijwayelekile (ikheli le-IP, i-user-agent, isikhathi). Umthuthukisi akayitholi leyo thrafikhi.

Ukufakwa okuvela ku-Microsoft Store akusebenzisi lokhu kuhlola; i-Store iletha izibuyekezo.

## Ishejuli

Ku-Windows ungasebenzisa umsebenzi uma ungena noma ngeshejuli yeviki. Uhlelo luyibhalisa ku-Windows Task Scheduler ngagama eliqala ngo-`BUtil`. Lokhu kuqala lelo hlelo kuphela kukhompyutha yakho.

## Izingane

Uhlelo liyithuluzi lokwenza amakhophi nokuhlanganisa amafayela. Alibhekiswe ezinganeni ezingaphansi kweminyaka engu-13.

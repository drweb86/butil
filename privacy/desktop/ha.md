# Manufar sirri

An sabunta karshe: 25 Satumba 2026

**BUtil** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Sunan aikace-aikacen: BUtil
Sunan mai haɓakawa: Siarhei Kuchuk

BUtil yana yin ajiyar fayiloli, yana daidaita su, kuma yana dawo da su a wannan kwamfuta. Hakanan yana iya shigo da midiya, raba folda, ko loda fayiloli zuwa uwar garken da kuka saita. Ba ya ƙirƙirar asusun mai haɓakawa. Mai haɓakawa ba ya gudanar da uwar garke da ke karɓar fayilolinku, kalmomin sirri, ko bayanan amfani.

## Bayanan da mai haɓakawa ba ya tattarawa

Aikace-aikacen ba shi da talla, nazarin bayanai, rahotannin rugujewa, ko SDK na bin sawu. Mai haɓakawa ba ya tattara, sayarwa, ko raba bayanan sirri.

## Bayanan da aka adana a kwamfutarku

### Ayyuka da saituna

Ma'anoni na ayyuka ana adana su a wannan kwamfuta kawai. Aiki na iya ƙunshe da hanyoyin folda, jadawali, saitunan ajiya, da kalmomin sirri ko token da kuka rubuta. Ana ɓoye kalmomin sirri da asirin ajiya a wannan kwamfuta kafin a adana su, kuma ana iya karanta su a wannan kwamfuta kawai. Ba a tura waɗannan ƙimomi zuwa mai haɓakawa ba.

- Ayyukan Windows: `%AppData%\BUtil Backup Tasks`
- Ayyukan Linux: `~/.config/BUtil Backup Tasks`
- Saitunan Windows (ciki har da jigo da harshen da aka zaɓa na ƙarshe don lasisi ko sirri): `%AppData%\BUtil\Settings\v1`
- Saitunan Linux: `~/.config/BUtil/Settings/v1`
- Yanayin ayyukan Windows: `%AppData%\BUtil\States`
- Yanayin ayyukan Linux: `~/.config/BUtil/States`
- Yanayin shigo da midiya na Windows: `%AppData%\BUtil Backup Tasks - States`
- Yanayin shigo da midiya na Linux: `~/.config/BUtil Backup Tasks - States`

### Fayilolin da kuka zaɓa

Ajiya, daidaitawa, dawowa, da shigo da kaya suna karantawa da rubuta foldodin da kuka zaɓa. Waɗannan fayiloli suna zama a wannan kwamfuta ko a wurin ajiyar da kuka saita. Aikace-aikacen ba ya tura su zuwa mai haɓakawa.

### Rijistoci

Ana rubuta rijistocin bincike a wannan kwamfuta kawai:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\BUtil\logs\v4`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/BUtil/logs/v4`

Ba a tura waɗannan fayiloli zuwa ko'ina ba.

Ba a amfani da uwar garken mai haɓakawa don adana bayananku ba.

## Amfani da hanyar sadarwa

### Wuraren da kuka saita

Lokacin da aiki ke gudana, aikace-aikacen yana haɗawa da wurin da kuka ƙayyade kawai. Wannan na iya zama folda na gida ko uwar garken da kuka shigar: FTP, FTPS, SFTP, WebDAV, SMB, NFS, ajiya mai dacewa da S3, ko Azure Blob Storage. Sunayen fayil, abin da ke ciki, da takardun shaidar da kuka shigar ana tura su zuwa wannan uwar garke domin aiki ya iya gudana. Kowace sabis tana da manufar sirri nata. Mai haɓakawa ba ya karɓar wannan zirga-zirga.

BUtil Server na iya sauraro a wannan kwamfuta domin abokin ciniki na BUtil da kuka saita ya iya tura fayiloli. Wannan zirga-zirga yana zama tsakanin kwamfutocin da kuka saita.

### Duba sabuntawa

Gina waɗanda ba na Store ba na iya neman sabuwar fitarwar GitHub:

`https://api.github.com/repos/drweb86/butil/releases/latest`

GitHub (Microsoft) yana karɓar buƙatar HTTPS ta al'ada (adireshin IP, user-agent, lokaci). Mai haɓakawa ba ya karɓar wannan zirga-zirga.

Shigarwa daga Microsoft Store ba sa amfani da wannan dubawa; Store ke kawo sabuntawa.

## Jadawali

A Windows kuna iya gudanar da aiki lokacin shiga ko bisa jadawalin mako-mako. Aikace-aikacen yana yin rijista a Windows Task Scheduler da suna da ke farawa da `BUtil`. Wannan yana ƙaddamar da wannan aikace-aikacen a kwamfutarku kawai.

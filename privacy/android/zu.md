# Inqubomgomo yobumfihlo

Igcine ukubuyekezwa: 21 Okthoba 2026

**BUtil** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Igama lohlelo lokusebenza: BUtil
Igama lomthuthukisi: Siarhei Kuchuk

I-BUtil igcina ikhophi, ivumelanisa, futhi ibuyisela amafayela kule divayisi. Ingaphinda ingenise imidiya, yabelane ngefolda nge-FTPS, noma ilayishe amafayela kuseva oyilungisayo. Ayidali i-akhawunti yomthuthukisi. Umthuthukisi akasebenzisi ingemuva elamukela amafayela akho, amaphasiwedi, noma idatha yokusetshenziswa.

## Idatha umthuthukisi angayiqoqi

Uhlelo aluna izikhangiso, ukuhlaziya, ababiki bokuphahlazeka, noma ama-SDK okulandelela. Umthuthukisi akaqoqi, akadayisi, futhi akabelani ngedatha yomuntu siqu.

Le nqubomgomo yobumfihlo nelayisensi kuboniswa ngaphakathi kohlelo. Azivulwa njengamakhasi ewebhu.

## Idatha egcinwe kudivayisi yakho

### Imisebenzi nezilungiselelo

Izincazelo zemisebenzi zigcinwa kuphela esitoleni esiyimfihlo sohlelo kule divayisi. Umsebenzi ungafaka izindlela zamafolda, ishejuli, izilungiselelo zokugcina, namaphasiwedi noma amathokheni owathayiphayo. Amaphasiwedi nezimfihlo zokugcina zivalwa nge-keystore ye-Android ngaphambi kokugcinwa, futhi zingafundwa kuphela kule divayisi. Lawo manani awalayishelwa kumthuthukisi. Awafakwa ekugcineni kwe-Android. Ukukhipha uhlelo kuyawasusa.

### Amafayela owakhethayo

Ukugcina ikhophi, ukuvumelanisa, ukubuyisela, ukungenisa, nokulayisha kufunda futhi kubhale amafolda owakhetha ngesikhethi samadokhumenti sesistimu, noma iseva oyilungisayo. Lawo mafayela ahlala emafodeni owakhethile, noma endaweni yokugcina oyilungisayo. Uhlelo aluwalayisheli kumthuthukisi. Ukukhipha uhlelo akususi amafayela akumafolda owakhethile.

### Isitifiketi se-FTPS

Uma wabelana ngefolda nge-FTPS, uhlelo ludala isitifiketi esizisayinele esitoleni esiyimfihlo sohlelo. Ukukhipha uhlelo kuyasisusa leso sitifiketi. Amafayela abiwe ahlala efodeni oyivumele.

Asikho iseva lomthuthukisi elisetshenziselwa ukugcina idatha yakho.

## Ukusetshenziswa kwenethiwekhi

### Izindawo ozilungisayo

Uma umsebenzi usebenza, uhlelo luxhuma kuphela endaweni oyibekayo. Lokho kungaba ifolda kule divayisi, noma iseva oyifakayo: FTP, FTPS, SFTP, WebDAV, SMB, NFS, isitoreji esihambisana ne-S3, noma i-Azure Blob Storage. Amagama amafayela, okuqukethwe amafayela, nezitifiketi zokungena ozifakile zithunyelwa kuleyo seva ukuze umsebenzi ukwazi ukusebenza. Lonke lalawo masevisi linomthetho walo wobumfihlo. Umthuthukisi akayitholi leyo thrafikhi.

Ukwabelana ngefolda nge-FTPS kulalela kule divayisi ukuze iklayenti ye-FTPS oyilungisayo ikwazi ukufunda noma ukubhala ifolda oyivumele, isebenzisa igama lomsebenzisi nephasiwedi oyibekayo. Leyo thrafikhi ihlala phakathi kwamadivayisi akho (nanoma ubani enethiwekhini onalezo zitifiketi zokungena). Umthuthukisi akayona ingxenye yayo. Wena unomthwalo wokuthi ubani angafinyelela embobeni, iyiphi ifolda oyabelana ngayo, nokuthi lelo phasiwedi liqinile kangakanani.

Uhlelo aluhloli izibuyekezo futhi aluxhumani nesevisi yokubuyekeza.

## Ishejuli

Ungasebenzisa umsebenzi uma idivayisi iqala, noma ngezinsuku ozikhethayo. Uhlelo lubhalisa lokho newashi le-alamu lesistimu. Lokho kuqala lolu hlelo kuphela kudivayisi yakho.

## Izimvume

Uhlelo lusebenzisa lezi zimvume kuphela:

- **I-inthanethi** kanye **nesimo senethiwekhi** ukuze kufinyelelwe iseva oyilungisayo nokwabelana ngefolda nge-FTPS.
- **Izaziso** kanye **nesevisi yangaphambili** ukuze umsebenzi osebenzayo uqhubeke uma isikrini sicishiwe.
- **Ama-alamu anembile** kanye **nokusebenza ekuqaleni** ukuze kuqale umsebenzi ohleliwe.
- **Ukukhiya kokuvusa** ukuze kuncishiswe ukulala ngesikhathi sokudlulisa.

## Izingane

Uhlelo luyithuluzi lokugcina ikhophi nokuvumelanisa amafayela. Alibhekiswe ezinganeni ezingaphansi kweminyaka engu-13.

## Abanye

Abanikezeli bokugcina obalungisayo bacubungula amafayela nezitifiketi zokungena umsebenzi abathumela zona. Umthuthukisi akayitholi leyo thrafikhi.

## Izinguquko

Izibuyekezo zale nqubomgomo zifakiwe ohlelweni.

<p>BUtil backs up, syncs, and restores files. Incremental backups store only what changed. Deduplication keeps a single copy when the same file appears more than once.</p>
<p><b>Where the files go</b></p>
<p>Send backups to a folder, a NAS (SMB/CIFS), or to WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, NFS, Azure Blob, SFTP, or FTPS.</p>
<ul>
<li>Sync a folder through storage you control, and keep older versions when files change.</li>
<li>Import pictures, video, and audio from an SD card or a camera.</li>
<li>Run tasks on a schedule, including from the console.</li>
</ul>
<p>Passwords you save are encrypted on the device. There is no account, no advertising, and no telemetry.</p>
<p>BUtil is open source. Its own code is dedicated to the public domain. Third-party components keep their own licenses.</p>

### Nkwupụta ebumnuche

Iwu ọtụtụ mba na-enye onye okike na ndị nwe ya ikike nwebisiinka na ikike metụtara ya ozugbo.

Ụfọdụ ndị nwe chọrọ ịhapụ ikike ndị ahụ kpamkpam ka e jiri ọrụ ahụ wuo, gbanwee, tinyekọta, jikwaa ọzọ ma kesaa n'enweghị ụjọ, gụnyere azụmahịa.

Onye jikọtara CC0 («Onye kwupụtara») na-eme ya n'ọnụ ọnụ ya n'enweghị ụgwọ ọzọ.

### 1. Nwebisiinka na ikike metụtara ya

Ọrụ n'okpuru CC0 nwere ike ịnwe nchebe. Ha gụnyere:

- mmepụtagharị, mmegharị, nkesa, ngosi, ntụgharị
- ikike omume nke onye ode/onye nka
- ikike ọha na nzuzo nke ihu mmadụ
- nchebe megide asọmpi na-ezighị ezi (4(a))
- iwepụta data na ijikwa ọzọ
- ikike nchekwa data (Directive 96/9/EC)
- ikike yiri ya n'ụwa niile

### 2. Ịhapụ

N'ókè iwu kwere, Onye kwupụtara na-ahapụ nwebisiinka n'ihu ọha, kpamkpam, ruo mgbe ebighị ebi, nke a na-apụghị ịkagbu, n'enweghị ọnọdụ: n'ụwa niile, oge kachasị, ọwa ọ bụla, ebumnuche ọ bụla gụnyere azụmahịa. Ọ bụ maka ọdịmma ọha ma a gaghị ekagbu ya.

### 3. Ikike ọha nke ndabere

Ọ bụrụ na ịhapụ adịghị irè, a ga-echekwa ya ruo ókè kachasị. A na-enyekwa ikike n'efu, nke a na-apụghị ịnyefe, nke a na-apụghị inye n'okpuru, nke na-abụghị naanị otu onye, nke a na-apụghị ịkagbu. Akụkụ adịghị irè anaghị emebi ndị ọzọ.
